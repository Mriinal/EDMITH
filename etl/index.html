ETL Testing & Data Warehouse Quality

Why this skill is mandatory: Modern enterprises process petabytes of raw transactional events into mission-critical analytical warehouses every hour. If flawed records slip in, executive revenue dashboards fail and regulatory penalties follow. ETL Testers are the essential guardians of data truth—validating complete extraction, schema transformations, historical accuracy with Slowly Changing Dimensions (SCD), and executing automated SQL reconciliation suites.
